## Not automatically included
- A file being accessible to you through a university library does **not** mean the publisher PDF can be redistributed on your personal website.
- Author-accepted manuscripts can often be shared under publisher-specific rules; confirm the journal's agreement, embargo, and required notices first.
- For articles without a specifically verified open manuscript or repository, the website retains only the official journal DOI.
- OSF project view-only links should be reviewed by the project team before launch, including whether data should still be public and whether rights to redistribute code or materials are clear.

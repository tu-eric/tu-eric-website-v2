02 / Methods & Skills

Quantitative methods
& skills.

Many psychological questions involve constructs we cannot observe directly, people whose responses are linked, or experiences that change over time. I use quantitative methods to address these challenges in relationship science and collaborative mental health research.

Quantitative approach

Choosing an analysis starts with the design.

A speed-dating study contains overlapping impressions, couples provide interdependent reports, and longitudinal research follows the same people over time. Those structures matter for what a model can estimate and what its results mean. I select methods around the design and the substantive question, not the other way around.

I use R for data cleaning, psychometric analyses, multilevel and structural equation models, simulation, visualization, and reproducible reporting. I also develop methodological research, teach statistics, and help collaborators interpret findings.

Four areas of methodological practice

Methods in practice.

01 / Measurement

Psychometrics & scale development

Exploratory and confirmatory factor analysis, item response theory, reliability and validity assessment, and measurement invariance across groups and partners.

In practice

For the RoCh Scale, I combined item generation and reduction with factor analysis, item response methods, and tests of measurement structure across single people, partnered people, and couples.

02 / Interdependence

Dyadic & structural equation modeling

Social Relations Models, actor–partner and common-fate models, latent dyadic SEM, and ways of accounting for measurement error and shared variation.

In practice

I used the Social Relations Model in speed-dating research and co-authored a review of latent dyadic SEM. With John Sakaluk, I am also developing Monte Carlo simulations comparing ways to represent individual and shared experiences.

View methods publication ↗
03 / Change over time

Multilevel & longitudinal analysis

Multilevel models, repeated-measures designs, intensive daily diaries, and longitudinal associations in cohort and dyadic data.

In practice

I have analyzed 21-day diary data from romantic couples and longitudinal mental health data at SickKids, including studies of adolescent depression and cognitive functioning.

04 / Implementation

R, reproducibility & reporting

Data cleaning, reproducible R workflows, diagnostic checks, simulation, visualization, and clear reporting of effect sizes and uncertainty.

In practice

At SickKids, I conduct analyses, interpret findings, and draft Methods and Results sections. I have also developed and evaluated dating-matching algorithms for the Aphrodite Project and taught R through statistics courses and workshops.

Methodological scholarship

Research on statistical practice.

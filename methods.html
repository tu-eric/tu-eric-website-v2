02 / Methods & Skills

Quantitative methods
& skills.

I work with statistical methods that help answer questions about measurement, relationships between people, and change over time. My work includes methodological publications, applied analyses, and teaching statistics in R.

Quantitative approach

Choosing an analysis starts with the design.

A study of two romantic partners calls for different assumptions than a survey of unrelated individuals. So does a study that follows the same person over weeks or years. I work with models suited to these designs, including dyadic, multilevel, and latent-variable approaches.

I use R for data management, statistical analysis, model checking, visualization, and reporting. I also teach statistics and programming and contribute to collaborative analyses.

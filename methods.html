02 / Methods & Skills

Better questions.
Stronger evidence.

My quantitative expertise spans psychological measurement, dyadic and longitudinal analysis, structural equation modeling, and reproducible programming. I apply these methods in both substantive and methodological scholarship.

Quantitative approach

Method is part of the question.

Good psychological research depends on a close fit between theory, design, and statistical analysis. I work with repeated observations, interdependent people, latent constructs, and complex data structures—and translate those analyses into clear, interpretable results.

My work spans independent research, methodological publications, statistical consulting, applied research collaborations, and the teaching of statistics using R.

03 / Teaching

Learning by doing.

I teach students to reason with evidence and apply statistical tools to meaningful psychological questions. My experience includes independent statistics instruction, R workshops, mentoring, and teaching in social and relationship psychology.

Instructor of record

Statistical Methods II
PSYC 2022

I taught Statistical Methods II at York University in Winter 2024 and Winter 2026. The course guides students through inference, analysis in R, and clear interpretation of statistical results.

My teaching emphasizes connecting each method to a meaningful psychological question, with structured opportunities to practice, troubleshoot, and communicate findings.

Teaching philosophy

Clarity, practice,
confidence.

My aim is for students to leave the classroom able to understand why an analysis is appropriate, what it can tell us, and where its limitations lie.

02

Improve through feedback

Use student questions and evaluations to adjust teaching while preserving academic rigor.

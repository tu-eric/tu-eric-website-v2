02 / My research questions

From first impressions
to longer-term outcomes.

Why does chemistry emerge with some people but not others? What changes when we follow the same couple over time? And what can longitudinal data tell us about young people’s mental health?

These questions call for different designs, including dyadic studies, daily diaries, measurement development, and longitudinal analyses.

Featured independent research / Romantic Chemistry Scale

What do we mean by chemistry?

My dissertation develops and evaluates the Romantic Chemistry (RoCh) Scale. Rather than treating chemistry as a single spark, the measure examines five dimensions of how people experience connection with a current or potential romantic partner.

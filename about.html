04 / About

Hello, I’m Eric.

I’m a PhD candidate in Social and Personality Psychology at York University and a Data Analyst at the SickKids Research Institute. My main research interests are romantic chemistry and relationship development. I also work on quantitative methods and collaborative youth mental health research.

I study romantic connection and the methods needed to understand it. I also bring quantitative expertise to collaborative work on child and adolescent mental health.
